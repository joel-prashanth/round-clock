    Four 2-minute rounds with 30 seconds of rest. Read the focus line on the clock each round.

      Stance
      • Left foot forward, right foot back, about shoulder-width apart. If you're left-handed, mirror everything.
      • Back heel slightly raised, knees soft, weight about even on both feet.
      • Hands up near your cheekbones, elbows tucked in, chin down.
      Footwork: step and slide
      • Move in the direction you want to go with the nearest foot first, then slide the other foot after it.
      • Never cross your feet or bring them together. Keep the shoulder-width gap.
      • Practise forward, back, left and right in small steps.
      The jab
      • Shoot the front hand straight out from your guard and turn the fist palm-down as you arrive.
      • Snap it back along the same line to your guard. Don't lock the elbow out hard.
      • Breathe out sharply on every punch and keep your back hand up the whole time.
      Mistakes to watch
      • Dropping your back hand.
      • Leaning forward when you punch.
      • Flaring your elbows out.
      • Crossing your feet.
      • Holding your breath.
      • Prop your phone up and film one round to check your form.
